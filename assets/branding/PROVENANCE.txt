Typstographe st icon

Source font: EB Garamond Italic, TeX Live ebgaramond distribution.
Upstream: https://github.com/octaviopardo/EBGaramond12
Glyph: uniFB06, selected by HarfBuzz for st with dlig=1.
Native bounds: (-28, -14, 618, 671), units per em: 1000.
Original uniform normalization: scale 0.510836, SVG translation (105.303406, 423.809598), Y axis inverted for SVG.
Approved enlargement: 1.15 about (256, 256), relative to the coloured plate.
No contour edits, rotation, synthetic slant, or added stroke.
The icon uses the original italic orientation; the 25-degree rotation belongs to the wordmark and splash.

Master outline: ligature-st.svg (self-contained SVG, currentColor).
Font license retained for provenance: licenses/EB-Garamond-OFL.txt.
The font itself is not bundled by this icon export.

WORDMARK AND SPLASH
Typ and ographe use EB Garamond Regular from the same distribution and license.
The original italic st glyph is inserted with a uniform scale and a 25-degree
counterclockwise rotation in font coordinates. Native letter contours are unchanged.
wordmark.svg is the static master. strokes.json stores the pen trajectories and
approved timing; generate.py derives stroke-data.mjs and splash.svg. reveal.mjs
preserves the corrected bridge/turn join at (491,599). All graphical surfaces
share motion.mjs and the same reveal geometry.

DOCUMENT ICON
The document silhouette is application artwork generated by generate.py. Its
st mark reuses ligature-st.svg without contour edits.
