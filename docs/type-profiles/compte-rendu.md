# Meeting minutes

ID: `compte-rendu` · Status: **Planned — not creatable** · Groups: Correspondence and administration.

Record a meeting, its participants, discussions and decisions.

This is a product profile, not an executable metadata or Typst signature.
For available types, the implemented contract and capability catalogue remain authoritative.
For planned types, the following needs are provisional and require joint validation before coding.

## Information to capture

- Meeting title, date and place or remote meeting information.
- Writer, participants and apologies.

## Capability needs

- Rich text, lists, quotations, images and tables.
- Decisions and action items when their contract is agreed.

## First useful model

Readable personal meeting minutes with clear meeting information.

## Decisions and open questions

- Decide whether decisions and actions need dedicated structured records.
