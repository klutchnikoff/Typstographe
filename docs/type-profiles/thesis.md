# Dissertation and thesis

ID: `thesis` · Status: **Planned — not creatable** · Groups: Research and publications.

Compose a long academic work with chapters, references, appendices and examination information.

This is a product profile, not an executable metadata or Typst signature.
For available types, the implemented contract and capability catalogue remain authoritative.
For planned types, the following needs are provisional and require joint validation before coding.

## Information to capture

- Titles, author, institution, qualification and discipline.
- Supervisors, optional examination panel and defense date.
- Abstracts and keywords by language.

## Capability needs

- Chapters, contents, figures and tables with captions.
- Footnotes, citations, bibliography, appendices and cross-references.

## First useful model

A neutral academic manuscript with a title page and readable chapters.

## Decisions and open questions

- One type covers both dissertation and thesis initially.
- Agree front matter and optional examination information before implementation.
