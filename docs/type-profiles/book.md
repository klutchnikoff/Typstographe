# Book

ID: `book` · Status: **Planned — not creatable** · Groups: Research and publications, Books and communication.

Organize a book into parts and chapters with contents, notes and appendices.

This is a product profile, not an executable metadata or Typst signature.
For available types, the implemented contract and capability catalogue remain authoritative.
For planned types, the following needs are provisional and require joint validation before coding.

## Information to capture

- Titles, authors or editors and language.
- Optional publication information, dedication and acknowledgements.

## Capability needs

- Parts, chapters, contents, figures, tables, notes and cross-references.
- Bibliography and appendices; index and glossary to assess.

## First useful model

A restrained long-form book manuscript.

## Decisions and open questions

- Agree parts, front matter and back matter before implementation.
- Distinguish a book from a thesis through its editorial metadata and contract.
