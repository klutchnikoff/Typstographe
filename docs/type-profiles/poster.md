# Scientific poster

ID: `poster` · Status: **Planned — not creatable** · Groups: Research and publications, Books and communication.

Present research, key findings and references visually on a large page.

This is a product profile, not an executable metadata or Typst signature.
For available types, the implemented contract and capability catalogue remain authoritative.
For planned types, the following needs are provisional and require joint validation before coding.

## Information to capture

- Title, authors, affiliations and optional event.
- Optional contact details and acknowledgements.

## Capability needs

- Semantic sections, figures, tables, equations and bibliography.
- A model-controlled multi-column composition.

## First useful model

A restrained scientific poster with a clear reading order.

## Decisions and open questions

- Define the relationship between sections and model-controlled spatial composition.
- Agree useful page sizes and accessible reading order.
