# Report

ID: `report` · Status: **Planned — not creatable** · Groups: Correspondence and administration, Research and publications, Books and communication.

Structure an activity report, a study or an internship report with appendices and references when needed.

This is a product profile, not an executable metadata or Typst signature.
For available types, the implemented contract and capability catalogue remain authoritative.
For planned types, the following needs are provisional and require joint validation before coding.

## Information to capture

- Title, subtitle, writers, organization and date.
- Optional reporting period and summary.

## Capability needs

- Sections, contents, figures, tables, footnotes and bibliography.
- Appendices and cross-references.

## First useful model

A sober one-column report suitable for everyday professional use.

## Decisions and open questions

- Keep activity, study and internship reports together initially.
- Distinguish its heading and contract from Article and Thesis.
