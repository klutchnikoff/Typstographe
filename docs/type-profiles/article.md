# Scientific article

ID: `article` · Status: **Available** · Groups: Research and publications.

Write an article with authors, affiliations, multilingual abstracts, citations and an automatic bibliography.

This is a product profile, not an executable metadata or Typst signature.
For available types, the implemented contract and capability catalogue remain authoritative.
For planned types, the following needs are provisional and require joint validation before coding.

## Information to capture

- Titles, subtitles and short titles by language.
- Authors, free-text affiliations, corresponding author and optional ORCID.
- Abstracts and keywords by language.
- Bibliography settings and optional contents.

## Capability needs

- Rich text, sections, lists, quotations, images, tables and footnotes.
- Bibliographic citations and automatic bibliography.

## First useful model

A restrained one-column working article.

## Decisions and open questions

- All Article models remain interchangeable.
- Models may leave presentation options free, restrict them or impose them without changing content.
